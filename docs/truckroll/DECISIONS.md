| D-37 | Pins that can't be honoured. **Decided by Shane 2026-11-14 (11:06 AM CT, "go D-15 D-17"): pinned jobs are never dropped or moved; supersedes D-17** | (a) keep D-17: a pin that can't be honoured comes back unassigned with a reason; (b) fail the whole solve with a pin-conflict error naming each pin at fault; (c) drop the pin itself (move the job) and warn | **(b)**. Before the search the solver judges every pin (`pin_conflicts` in `solve.py`): `no-skill`, `position-over-capacity` (the pinned position is past the tech's `maxJobs`, which used to crash OR-Tools), `no-window-fit`, `position-taken`, `over-capacity` (more pins than `maxJobs`), then, only if none of those apply, `infeasible-together` (each pin fits alone but no route holds them all; a bounded exact search, 100,000 prefixes per tech, else `search-limit`).
